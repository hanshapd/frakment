Official visual resources for referencing Frakment in articles, products, or projects.

## About

**Frakment** is a monochrome component registry for AI and chat interfaces. Components are copy-paste, standalone, and dependency-light — designed to sit quietly next to whatever product they're embedded in.

The brand reflects that intent: neutral, typographic, and without decoration. Ink on paper. Paper on ink. Nothing that competes with the interface itself.

## Usage

Give the mark room to breathe. Scale it up or down as needed, but never stretch, recolor, outline, rotate, or layer effects on top of it. Keep at least the height of the logomark as clear space on every side.

## Logomark

Use the logomark for tight layouts, logo-only grids, or places where text alongside the mark would feel cramped. Monochrome usage is preferred — dark version on light surfaces, light version on dark surfaces. Use at **16px minimum**.

## Colors

The Frakment palette is **high-contrast monochrome** so it stays consistent across light and dark mode. Click any hex value to copy it.

<div className="not-prose my-6 grid grid-cols-2 gap-3 sm:grid-cols-4">
  <BrandColorSwatch name="Frakment Ink" hex="#0A0A0A" rgb="10, 10, 10" />
  <BrandColorSwatch name="Frakment Slate" hex="#737373" rgb="115, 115, 115" />
  <BrandColorSwatch name="Frakment Mist" hex="#A3A3A3" rgb="163, 163, 163" />
  <BrandColorSwatch name="Frakment Paper" hex="#FFFFFF" rgb="255, 255, 255" />
</div>

Body text and headings use **Ink** on light surfaces, **Paper** on dark surfaces. Muted text, borders, and disabled states use **Slate** and **Mist**. Color is reserved for state — errors, warnings, success — never for decoration.
