Chain of Thought is a summary line that expands into the steps an agent took. The summary says what happened, like "Researched in 3 steps". The steps sit inside it, each one a line of its own.

```tsx title="example.tsx"

  ChainOfThought,
  ChainOfThoughtHeader,

  ChainOfThoughtStep,
```

## Steps

## Accessibility

- **TraceIcon** is `aria-hidden`. **TraceTitle** is the accessible name. Write it as a sentence: "Searched for minimumReleaseAge", not just "minimumReleaseAge".
- Status is conveyed by icon shape and color, not color alone.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: shimmer stops automatically.
- Use `open={open ?? running}` so a reader's manual toggle wins over the auto state.
